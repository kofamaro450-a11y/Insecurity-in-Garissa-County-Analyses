# Layer A — Original Empirical Research

**Assessment of Kenya's Current Insecurity Challenges: A Case Study of Security
Challenges in Garissa County**
Undergraduate thesis, BA Political Science, The Catholic University of Eastern
Africa (2023).

This is the original, self-contained social-science research this project is
built on. It stands on its own — everything in `layer-b-computational-extension/`
is a later addition, not a prerequisite for reading this.

## What's here

`Garissa_Insecurity_Thesis.docx` — the full thesis, including:

- A genuine research question (drivers of insecurity in Garissa County)
- Original fieldwork: **397 valid survey responses**, **20 key-informant
  interviews**, and **4 focus group discussions**
- A documented sampling methodology (multi-stage, quota-based across five
  sub-counties) and instrument reliability testing
- Qualitative thematic analysis of root causes, government response, and
  proposed strategies
- An established conceptual framework situating the findings in the broader
  literature on conflict and insecurity in northeastern Kenya

The document also carries two later additions relevant to Layer B: a data-quality
note on a table-count inconsistency in the original submission, and a short
pointer (§A.5) to this repository.
